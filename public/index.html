What the prices mean

EN English: TCGplayer

Market is TCGplayer's Market Price, based on recent completed sales. Many $1,000+ manga rares sell rarely, so when there is no Market Price the tracker shows the lowest current listing instead, marked “listed”.

Data comes from tcgcsv.com, a free daily mirror of TCGplayer prices, refreshed once a day around 20:00 UTC.

JP Japanese: Yuyu-tei (遊々亭)

Sell is the shop's asking price. Manga rares are almost always sold out, and Yuyu-tei then keeps showing the last asking price, so a sold-out sell price can be stale.

Buy is what Yuyu-tei pays to buy the card from you. It stays current even when the card is out of stock, so it is the better live signal for Japanese prices.

Currency and “Japan premium”

Yen are converted at the daily ECB reference rate (via frankfurter.dev). The Japan premium compares Yuyu-tei's sell price with the English price. Positive means the Japanese copy costs more.

PRB marks Premium Booster reprints, which TCGplayer and Yuyu-tei list separately from the original print. Red marks OP13's red manga variants.
